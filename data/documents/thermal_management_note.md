# Thermal Management Note

Source type: project internal note.
Published at: 2026.
Category: thermal_management.

Hot aisle containment and cold aisle containment reduce air recirculation by separating supply air from return air. In a data-center-cooling style scenario, containment evidence should be combined with zone temperature trends before making an operational diagnosis.

When local hot spots persist, the system should inspect zone temperature, airflow assumptions, internal load, and recent control action changes instead of relying on a single average temperature.
